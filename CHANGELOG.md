# Changelog

All notable changes to Bobert: Kiro Credit Tracker. Bobert is an unofficial project. Versions with an odd minor number are beta (pre-release) builds; versions with an even minor number (1.0.x) are stable releases; see [versioning](docs/release-process.md#versioning).

Bobert is the new name of Cyclegauge; this is its first release under the new name. Settings and history from Cyclegauge are not carried over. If you had turned off session hints (`bobert.sessionHints`), budget warnings (`bobert.budgetWarnings`) or support prompts (`bobert.supportPrompts`) in Cyclegauge, they are on again in Bobert; turn them off again in your user settings. Cyclegauge's stored history stays where it was until you delete it; see [data left by Cyclegauge](docs/privacy-and-storage.md#data-left-by-cyclegauge).

## 1.0.0 (stable, macOS, Windows, Ubuntu)

The first stable release. Bobert goes beyond counting credits to helping you keep them: it warns before your reported credits run out, values a projected overage in dollars next to the cost of the next larger plan, hints when a smaller plan would have been enough, finds files Kiro probably does not need (with their total size), reviews your Steering and Hooks for avoidable credit use, and adds an interactive dashboard with a cycle comparison, a results summary and a shareable cycle recap. Session hints can remind you when your latest session's context is nearly full and note when its model is listed above Auto's credit rate (observed on macOS; enabled on Windows and Linux but not yet verified natively). A manual workspace audit also runs the Steering and Hooks review. The Audit view is grouped by what to do (Exclude from Kiro, Load only when needed, Run only manually, Worth a look; groups start collapsed, it warns when `.kiroignore` is not yet listed in Agent Ignore Files, and it marks files with prepared, unsaved edits), and **Review changes…** shows every edit in the editor's change preview with the why and the alternatives before anything is prepared as unsaved edits for you to review and save. The Cost Advisor form in the credit view has been polished into a collapsible, themed **Change your plan answers** section. A **Report a Problem** command turns a fixed-codes-and-counts diagnostics report into a ready GitHub issue. Collector, store, projection, billing, forecast and Cost Advisor algorithms and data formats are preserved.

- **Settings.** `bobert.sourceRoot`, `bobert.storageRoot` and `bobert.sourceTimezone` are machine-scoped and blank by default. The others are all user-level (a workspace cannot change them). On by default: `bobert.budgetWarnings` (on), `bobert.budgetWarningPercent` (80, 0 turns only that notice off), `bobert.supportPrompts` (on), `bobert.auditReminderDays` (30, 0 = off) and `bobert.sessionHints` (on; macOS observed, Windows and Linux enabled but not yet verified natively). Off by default: `bobert.auditAutomatically` (`off`; `onOpen` or `onOpenAndChanges`) and `bobert.configReviewAutomatically` (`off`; `onOpen` or `onOpenAndChanges`).
- **What you will notice.** Budget notices, an occasional support prompt and (once, for a workspace that was never checked) an audit reminder can appear; session hints read Kiro's session metadata (see Privacy). A one-time walkthrough opens on first use. The credit view now runs one bundled script (see Privacy).
- **New saved state** is small and lives in the editor's own storage: dates and flags for prompts, budget notices, the walkthrough and the automatic-review offer, per-workspace audit dates and salted hashes of items you hid, and the two summary and recap keys described below. Nothing contains paths, amounts or identifiers. **Bobert: Reset Reminders and Saved Summary** clears the prompt, summary and recap state.

### Added

**Budget, cost and plan**

- **Budget warnings** inside Kiro: a short notice when a fresh, regular forecast (not a provisional early estimate) reaches your reported credit limit before the reset, when usage reaches `bobert.budgetWarningPercent` of it (default 80, 0 = off), or when the limit is reached. Each notice appears at most once per billing cycle and once per window, only the most severe when several are due, with **Show details** and **Turn off** buttons. `bobert.budgetWarnings` (user level, default on) turns them off. The status bar item uses the error colour when the limit is reached or a regular forecast says it will be before the reset; this colour belongs to the gauge and does not depend on the setting. Accounts with unsupported extra credits get no budget notices, and the support prompt does not appear in a window that showed a budget notice. The only state kept is the cycle and the notices already shown, in editor global storage, written before a notice is shown.
- A **modelled overage cost** in the credit view and in the forecast and limit notices: projected credits over the reported limit at the observed pace (and credits already above it at the last update), valued at the per-credit price of the reviewed price list, rounded to whole cents, with the price and the price list's review date (and version in the credit view). Shown only with a valid price list; a modelled scenario, not an invoice, and whether overage applies depends on your plan and settings. Left out for accounts with unsupported extra credits.
- **Next larger plan beside a projected overage**: the credit view adds "For comparison: Pro+ costs $20 a month more than Pro and includes 1000 more credits (list prices, price list reviewed …)" after the projected-overage line, and the forecast and limit notices that carry an overage cost append a short form. Only the two list-price differences from the reviewed price list; no recommendation and no saving claim. Nothing for Power, an unknown plan, accounts with extra credits or an invalid price list; an inferred plan is prefixed "If you are on …".
- **Plan right-sizing hint**, read-only on top of the Cost Advisor's 1-month comparison. When a smaller plan would have served both the forecast and the last completed billing cycle, the credit view says so, and a lowest-priority budget notice mentions it once per cycle in the last 7 days before the reset ("You will likely leave about N credits unused this cycle, and the last cycle also fit within Pro …"). The smaller plan must cover the projected usage, so the unused part is at least the gap between the plans. "Fit" means the last cycle's observed usage plus an allowance for its last unobserved hours, taken from stored history only where it is fully backed (three observed cycles); nothing is shown otherwise. Modelled, not advice or an invoice; moving to Free is never suggested.

**Interactive dashboard**

- The credit view is now an interactive dashboard: usage chart with hover and keyboard tooltips and forecast scenarios, today's pace (the observed net change within the UTC day, with its start and elapsed time, never extrapolated), a comparison with the previous billing cycle (shown only where both cycles have evidence-backed starts, otherwise bracketing observations or "not comparable"), live updates, and the Cost Advisor as an inline form with the same result as the native pickers (answers are still never persisted). The form sits in a collapsible **Change your plan answers** section, open by default while your plan is unconfirmed (your own open or closed choice is kept while the view is open), with the save confirmation visible below it; its fields, labels and buttons use the editor theme's dropdown, input and button colours and focus ring (light, dark and high contrast). The duplicate **Open Cost Advisor** button shows only as a fallback while the inline form is not available, and the "Not your plan?" hint points to the form. No new commands or links.
- **Your Bobert summary**: the exclusions you saved in the observed billing cycle (items, possible secrets, known bytes, shown as a lower bound when sizes are unknown) and an observed-use trend against the preceding period, built only from known facts. Gaps read "not observed" or "not comparable", never zero, and no "saved credits" are estimated.
- **Your Kiro cycle (cycle recap)**: when a new billing cycle is first observed and the previous one has an evidence-backed start and end, a dismissible card shows credits used, whether they lasted until the reset (yes only with below-limit usage seen in the last 6 hours before it, no only if usage at or above the limit was observed, otherwise unknown), the comparable trend, the exclusions you saved and the busiest observed UTC day, labelled as a partial observation. It is rendered locally with no identifiers, paths or workspace names; preview it, then **Save image…** (save dialog, guarded target) or **Copy text**. The Open VSX link on the card is left out when `bobert.supportPrompts` is off.
- **Export CSV…** of your own accepted history, through the save dialog: allowlisted columns, exact decimals, `unknown` instead of zero, no free text. A fixed message replaces the export when several or unknown accounts are present.

**Workspace audit**

- **Bobert: Run Workspace Audit** and the **Workspace Audit** view in the Explorer. Checks a trusted local workspace folder for files that could add cost or risk if Kiro reads them (secrets and credentials, databases and dumps, large archives, logs and data files, build output, dependencies and other large files) and whether the workspace ignore files already cover them. It runs when you start it, or automatically only if you opt in. It reads only file names and sizes plus the ignore files listed in Agent Ignore Files (`kiroAgent.agentIgnoreFiles`); it writes no files and sends nothing, and file contents are never read. Results that cannot be established are shown as unknown rather than guessed. The view is grouped by what Bobert suggests doing. A status line (time of the check, number of suggestions, size Kiro may read, when it checks again) is followed by the groups **Exclude from Kiro** (adds lines to `.kiroignore`; the categories are sub-groups), **Load only when needed**, **Run only manually** and **Worth a look** (no automatic change), then **Not checked**; items are sorted by size, largest first. Wording is plain: tooltips give the short why and the alternatives, entries say "Kiro may read this" or "Unclear", and every apply group's tooltip says "Nothing changes until you save." Groups start collapsed (see Changed). The group's check mark and each item's check mark do exactly the group's verb, under one name, **Review changes…**; items otherwise have **Not a problem** (filename findings; hidden per workspace, only salted hashes are stored) and **Show file**, and **Disable** is in a hook's right-click menu. **Exclude all** and **Copy .kiroignore lines** are on the Exclude group, and the view notes when a `.kiroignore` exists but is not active because Agent Ignore Files does not list it. The one **Check** button runs the audit and the Steering and Hooks review.
- **Audit size total**: the view header shows the total size of the files Kiro may read but probably does not need (for example "about 240.3 MiB"), and the Workspace audit card shows it after the count (for example "4 items Kiro may read (about 240.3 MiB)"), with "at least" when some sizes are unknown or the list is cut short. Nested files are not counted twice. It is an exposure figure, not a saving or a measurement.
- A **Workspace audit** card in the credit view (item count and when it was last checked, no paths, no measured savings) with **Run audit** and **Show results** buttons, the **Bobert: Show Workspace Audit Results** command, and an **Open Cost Advisor** button at the end of the Cost Advisor section. The credit view's command links are limited to five Bobert commands (Cost Advisor, the two audit commands, Share and Report a Problem) through the webview's command allowlist, none with arguments.
- Audit reminders: a one-time prompt for a never-audited workspace and a reminder after `bobert.auditReminderDays` (default 30, 0 = off). Opt-in `bobert.auditAutomatically` (`off` by default; `onOpen` or `onOpenAndChanges`; the earlier `true` means `onOpen`) re-runs an audit that is at least one UTC day old silently when the workspace opens. `onOpenAndChanges` also watches the folder for new files matching the audit's own name patterns, re-checks a few seconds after they settle and shows at most one notice per window and UTC day, only when a new file is a secrets finding. Both are user-level settings only; per workspace only the last audit date, a snooze date and a do-not-remind flag are stored. Detection is by file name and size only. Findings are potential risks, not measured credit use; review suggestions before adding them, because ignoring files can hide them from Kiro.
- After a workspace has been checked, saving `.kiroignore` or a file named in Agent Ignore Files, or changing `kiroAgent.agentIgnoreFiles`, silently re-runs the check about a second later, so the audit view and the Workspace check card reflect the new rules.
- More audit name patterns: additional SSH and other private keys, keystores, password-manager databases, credential stores (`.netrc`, `.pgpass`, `.git-credentials`, `.htpasswd`, …), shell and database histories, Terraform state, VPN profiles and tool credential files (secrets); SQL Server and SQL dump files (databases); network captures and array/model data files (data); more build caches; `Pods`, `.yarn/cache`, `bower_components`, `jspm_packages`, `.terraform` and `.pnpm-store` (dependencies). `.ssh`, `.gnupg` and `.aws` folders are now listed as secrets instead of being skipped silently. Patterns are our own, informed by eth0izzle/shhgit (MIT), github-linguist/linguist (MIT) and github/gitignore (CC0); detection remains by name and size only.

**Steering and Hooks review**

- **Bobert: Review Kiro Steering and Hooks** (`bobert.reviewKiroConfig`), (also run by the Workspace Audit view's refresh button and available in the Command Palette) flags large always-included Steering files and broad, overlapping or agent-starting Hook triggers, with apply actions for narrower alternatives (see below). It runs when you use the command, and together with every manual workspace audit (the command, the Workspace Audit view button, the dashboard **Run audit** link, **Run audit** in the reminder, and **Show results** in the credit view when there is no result yet; not when you dismiss the folder picker or cancel the audit). It wins over an automatic run and does not count toward its limit; a failure of one does not hide the other's result. Silent audit re-runs never start it, and it needs a trusted workspace. It reads only root `AGENTS.md` (size), `.kiro/steering/*.md` (the first few bytes, up to 2 KiB only when the file starts with front matter) and `.kiro/hooks/*.json` (parsed up to 64 KiB per file) in up to eight trusted local roots; it executes nothing, shows no credit estimates and Bobert itself writes no workspace file.
- **Apply Steering and Hooks suggestions**: findings get actions that prepare an **unsaved edit in the real file**, like **Exclude from Kiro** (you review and save). **Review changes…** (**Load only when needed** for a large always-included Steering file, **Run only manually** for agent hooks, one item or a whole group) hands one edit with every change to the editor's change preview, each change annotated with the why and the alternatives and marked as needing confirmation, so you see the diff with a checkbox per change and Apply/Discard before anything changes. Nothing is saved by Bobert; after applying, a notice says "Changes prepared in M files — review and save.", and discarding changes nothing. In a group, an item whose guard fails (file changed, unsaved changes, auto save) is skipped and counted, and a hook with two findings is changed once. The preview depends on the editor's support for confirmed edits and has not yet been verified in Kiro; if the editor has no change preview, it applies the changes directly as unsaved edits (Bobert still never saves), so check the unsaved files before saving. **Narrow the pattern** (selects the `fileMatch` pattern value to type over), **Disable** (hook right-click menu) and **Open file** (large `AGENTS.md`) remain. Trusted workspaces only; the file must be unchanged since the review; with auto save on or unsaved changes nothing is prepared; if the exact place cannot be found safely (duplicate keys, unusual formatting, hard links) the file is opened without an edit. Combined-size and overlapping-hook findings keep a read-only **Show suggestion**. Files not encoded as UTF-8 may be re-encoded by the editor when you save, so check the change first.
- **Automatic review (opt-in)**: the user setting `bobert.configReviewAutomatically` (`off` by default, `onOpen`, `onOpenAndChanges`) runs the same review once per window shortly after a trusted workspace opens and, if chosen, after root `AGENTS.md`, `.kiro/steering/*.md` or `.kiro/hooks/*.json` change. Runs are debounced, at least 30 seconds apart and capped at 20 per window; the first run is silent and later runs show one fixed message only for a kind of finding not yet seen (at most three per window). After a manual review Bobert may offer once to turn it on; the offer respects `bobert.supportPrompts`, whose description mentions feature offers.

**Session hints (macOS observed, Windows and Linux enabled but not yet verified natively; on by default)**

- **Context reminder**: a non-modal notice when your most recent Kiro session in this workspace is about 70 % and again about 85 % full ("as of HH:MM"). The percentage is the latest value Kiro wrote for that session, a snapshot of the selected model's window, not Kiro's live meter. A threshold is armed again once the value falls 10 points below it (compaction, model switch). The thresholds are educated guesses, not evidence.
- **Model hint**: when your credit budget is tight (the status bar's warning or limit state) or the session is at least 30 % full, a notice that the session's model is listed by Kiro above Auto's credit rate ("Your Kiro credit budget is tight, and your most recent session in this workspace uses Claude Sonnet 4.5 (as of HH:MM), which Kiro lists at about 1.3x the credits of Auto (Kiro model table, as of 2026-10-18). For routine steps, Auto or a lighter model uses fewer credits; actual credits per task vary."), with **Show credit budget** and **Tips** buttons; **Tips** opens [Spend fewer Kiro credits](docs/credit-tips.md) on GitHub. Once per model per window; nothing is stored. It uses a dated table inside the extension, and is silent for Auto, unknown models and models at or below 1.0x. The table covers the 22 models of Kiro's published table (as of 2026-10-18); GPT-5.6 notices say "from about" because Kiro bills a higher factor above 272K tokens, and the retired Claude Sonnet 4.0 stays silent. Only Claude Sonnet 4.5 (and two cheaper MiniMax models) were seen in real session files; other identifiers come from Kiro's documentation or its published names and may be wrong or out of date, in which case no hint appears.
- Windows and Linux (maintainer decision 2026-10-18): same allowlisted fields, read-only, bounded, nothing persisted, local extension host only, Kiro only. Only macOS has observed evidence; the session folder (`.kiro/sessions` under your home folder) and the path format on Windows and Linux are not yet verified natively, so when the layout differs the feature stays silent. The workspace comparison follows the platform: case-insensitive on macOS and Windows (backslashes and a leading `/` before a drive letter normalised), case-sensitive on Linux.
- At most one notice per check, per session at most one in 30 minutes and three per run; only for sessions active after the window started and within about 10 minutes. The setting `bobert.sessionHints` (user level, default on) and the notice's **Turn off** button switch it off.

**Getting started, community and support**

- A **Get started with Bobert** walkthrough (command **Bobert: Get Started**) that opens once for new users inside Kiro: your credits, the workspace check (which needs no history), budget warnings, the session hints and when the forecast appears (about 2 days and 3 updates provisional, 7 days and 8 updates regular). Uses the packaged icon only and one global-state flag, `bobert.walkthrough.shown.v1`. Not yet verified in native Kiro; if the editor cannot open it, nothing happens.
- A quiet **footer** in the credit view with fixed links to rate Bobert on Open VSX, star it on GitHub and support it on Ko-fi, a **Share** link that runs **Bobert: Share Bobert** (copies a fixed text with the Open VSX link) and a **Report a problem** link. Links open only on click; no tracking parameters.
- A **support prompt** inside Kiro, at most once per window: about a minute after saving an **Exclude from Kiro** edit (at least one UTC day after first use), after opening the credit view on three distinct UTC days, or after completing a Cost Advisor review. The fallback remains about three minutes after start, at least seven UTC days after first use with a forecast or finding seen. Cancelled reviews and clearing answers do not count. **Rate** or **Support** pause it for 120 days; **Later** or dismissing for 60 days. The message points users to Bobert settings to turn off reminders. `bobert.supportPrompts` (user level, default on) turns it off; **Bobert: Reset Reminders and Saved Summary** clears all saved dates and flags. Existing users who chose the retired **Don't ask again** remain opted out through the legacy flag, which reset still clears. Prompt state remains in editor global storage; an explicit isolated synthetic-demo override permits native timing tests.
- Outside Kiro (and without `bobert.sourceRoot`), when no Kiro logs are found: a "Kiro not detected" status bar item and credit view message instead of "Collecting data", and a one-time note that Bobert is built for Kiro.

**Diagnostics and robustness**

- **Report a problem**: the command **Bobert: Report a Problem** (`bobert.reportProblem`) builds the same fixed-codes-and-counts report as Copy Diagnostics, opens it in an unsaved document, copies it and then opens Bobert's fixed GitHub bug-report form in the browser. Nothing is put in the address, nothing is sent by Bobert and nothing new is persisted. It is also offered as a **Report a problem** button on Bobert's own error notifications and as a footer link in the credit view.
- **Bobert: Copy Diagnostics** and CLI `--diagnostics`: a short report of fixed codes, counts and platform metadata to review and paste into an issue. Diagnostics also show `session_hints` (the last check's fixed code), `session_hint_notices` (a count) and `session_hint_capped` (a count of workspace folders skipped because they hold more than 200 sessions).
- A clear status-bar message on runtimes without SQLite ("Kiro: Needs a newer Kiro") and for data written by a newer Bobert ("Kiro: Update Bobert"), instead of a generic failure.

The editor requires an image per step and only the packaged icon is shipped, so each step keeps it with a step-specific description.

The two separate top-level notes are merged into it, and the success notice after **Review changes…** adds "Kiro uses .kiroignore only when it's listed in Agent Ignore Files." with an **Open setting** button.
- **Prepared, not saved**: items whose file holds a prepared, still unsaved edit read "prepared — save the file to apply" with a distinct icon, and the status line adds "· N files with unsaved prepared changes". The marks are kept in memory only and removed when the file is saved, closed or reverted.
- **Re-check after saving**: saving a Steering or Hook file that Bobert prepared runs one manual-equivalent Steering and Hooks review (same guards and limits, single-flight, not counted toward the automatic cap, no focus change). A save during a running review is answered by exactly one more review. Saving `.kiroignore` already re-checked the audit. No new watcher, no new storage, and nothing is read beyond the existing allowlist.
- **Exclude from Kiro** and the Steering and Hooks apply actions read the auto save setting with the opened file as scope, so a per-language auto save override also counts.
- The store has an ordered schema migration path (initially empty, store version 1). A store written by a newer Bobert is refused and never modified.
- Import evidence is bounded to 10,000 rows; unchanged codes refresh the latest row's time instead of adding rows. Pruning removes only old import-evidence rows, never snapshots or sources.
- The bundle loads SQLite lazily.
- The shared bounded file reader of the workspace audit was generalised (whole-file and tail modes); the audit's behaviour is unchanged.

### Privacy

- Everything runs locally: no telemetry, no sign-in and no network code. Kiro's files are only read, never changed. Feedback arrives only through the Report a Problem command, GitHub issues and Open VSX reviews.
- **Session hints** work on macOS (observed), Windows and Linux (enabled, not yet verified natively); local extension host only (no remote, WSL or SSH window), and only in Kiro. When the setting is off or in a remote window, no filesystem access happens at all. Bobert parses up to 20 of the most recently changed sessions' `session.json` files (each whole, in memory, including titles and workspace paths) to find the newest session of your open folder, then loads up to the last 256 KiB of that session's `messages.jsonl` (which can contain prompts and replies) into memory. It keeps only that session's model identifier, context percentage and time, compares workspace paths only to find your open folder, and never stores, logs, shows or sends anything else. Nothing is written; Kiro's files are opened read-only. The root (`~/.kiro/sessions`) is fixed; if `~/.kiro` is a symlink, the feature stays silent.
- The **workspace audit** reads only file names and sizes and the ignore files listed in Agent Ignore Files; the **Steering and Hooks review** (manual or automatic) reads exactly the allowlist above and nothing else. Only your user setting can enable the automatic modes, never a workspace, and the watcher handler reads no file and keeps no path. Bobert itself writes no workspace file: the apply actions open the real file and prepare an unsaved edit that you review and save. Its code reads no file text for them beyond what the review already reads (the Steering prefix of up to 2 KiB, Hook JSON up to 64 KiB); the editor loads the opened file as it does when you open it yourself. The review keeps, in memory only, a size, modification time and SHA-256 digest per file it read to prove the file is unchanged before an edit is prepared. Only combined-size and overlapping-hook findings still use untitled suggestion documents. The files in which an edit was prepared are tracked in memory only (at most 500 paths) while they are unsaved and are forgotten on save, close or revert. The change preview's why-and-alternatives text is fixed and holds no path or file content. The automatic review's offer is recorded in one global-state key, `bobert.configReview.offered.v1` (a boolean, no data, cleared by **Reset Support Prompts and Summary**).
- The credit view now runs **one bundled script** (nonce-based content security policy that blocks all network connections, validated message protocol) for the chart, the inline Advisor form, CSV export and recap actions. Its command links stay limited to five Bobert commands and its three fixed web links open only when clicked.
- The summary and recap keep one key each in the editor's global state, not the workspace and not Settings Sync (`bobert.results.v1`: at most the two most recent cycles with saved exclusions as counts and bytes; `bobert.recap.v1`: the cycle and a shown or dismissed flag). Saving the recap image or exporting CSV writes only a file you choose, after the save dialog, never inside Bobert's or Kiro's folders and never to a link or special file. Copy and save happen only when you click.

### Platform status

- macOS (Apple Silicon): earlier evidence under the previous name is synthetic-data testing in Kiro 1.2.56 and a candidate (audit, Copy Diagnostics, gauge) tested natively on macOS. macOS Intel is unverified.
- Windows 11 Pro and Ubuntu 26: earlier (under the previous name) smoke test of a development candidate in native Kiro by the maintainer (2026-10-14; Kiro versions not recorded). No itemised acceptance yet.
- RHEL is unverified. Session hints are observed on macOS only; on Windows and Linux (including Ubuntu) they are enabled but not yet verified natively, and stay silent if Kiro's session layout differs.
- Platforms and features verified natively for this release are recorded in [platform verification](docs/platform-verification.md). Newer features are covered by synthetic tests and CI; native verification of these is tracked in the [acceptance checklist](docs/validation/1.0-acceptance.md): the audit view wording, the Workspace audit card, the Exclude and hide actions, reminders and the watcher, the footer links, Share, Report a Problem (including the error-notification button and opening the form), the support prompt, the walkthrough, budget warnings, the overage and plan comparisons, the dashboard and recap, the Steering and Hooks review, session hints and the update-needed message and migration path; the "Kiro not detected" note has not been verified in any non-Kiro editor. The oldest Kiro version that works is not established; `engines.vscode` (`^1.90.0`) is an API claim, not a tested minimum.
